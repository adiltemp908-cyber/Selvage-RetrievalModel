Selvage

A ranked search engine over 100 clothing product descriptions, built on an lnc.ltc vector space model and a positional index.

lnc.ltc cosine similarity · top 10 results, ties broken by document ID
Two query forms, both evaluated with the positional index:
"cotton shirt" — exact phrase, terms must be adjacent and in order.
cotton WITHIN/3 shirt — ordered proximity, terms must occur in order within 3 token positions.

Look up any stemmed term to see its document frequency and full postings list — the same dictionary and positional index used to answer queries.
